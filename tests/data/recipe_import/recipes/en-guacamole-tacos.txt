Guacamole and Fresh Salsa for Tacos

Friday means tacos in our house, and the toppings matter more than the filling. Both of these can be made an hour ahead, just keep the guacamole covered tightly so it stays green. Serves 4.

Guacamole
2 ripe avocados
1 lime, juiced
1/2 red onion, finely chopped
a handful of coriander, chopped
a pinch of salt

Salsa
4 tomatoes, diced
1/4 red onion, finely chopped
1 lime, zest and juice
1 tbsp olive oil
8 small corn tortillas, to serve

Method
Mash the avocados with a fork, leaving a few chunks. Stir in the lime juice, the onion, the coriander and the salt. Press a sheet of cling film onto the surface.
For the salsa, mix the tomatoes, the onion, the lime zest and juice and the oil in a bowl. Let it stand for at least 15 minutes so the flavours come together.
Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, fill them with your favourite filling and top with a spoonful of each.
