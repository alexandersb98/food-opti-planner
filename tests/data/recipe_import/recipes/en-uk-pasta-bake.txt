Cheesy Tomato Pasta Bake

A proper midweek supper: nothing fancy, everything from the cupboard and one dish to wash. Serves 3-4, or two hungry people with leftovers for lunch.

You will need
250g penne
1 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, chopped
2 cloves garlic
400g tin chopped tomatoes
125g mozzarella, torn
salt and pepper to season

To serve
50g parmesan, grated
a small bunch of fresh basil

Method
1. Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Cook the penne in boiling salted water for two minutes less than the packet says, then drain.
2. While the pasta cooks, soften the onion in the oil for five minutes. Add the garlic for one more minute, then tip in the tomatoes and simmer for ten.
3. Stir the pasta into the sauce, season, and pour into an ovenproof dish. Scatter over the mozzarella.
4. Bake for 20 minutes until bubbling and golden at the edges.
5. Top with the parmesan and the basil leaves and bring the dish straight to the table.
