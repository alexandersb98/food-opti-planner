Chewy Oat and Chocolate Cookies

These are the cookies I bake when guests are coming and I have forty minutes. The dough does not need chilling, and the oats keep the middle soft for days (if they last that long). Makes 24 cookies.

Ingredients
1 stick butter, softened
3/4 cup brown sugar
1 egg
1 cup all-purpose flour
1 1/2 cups rolled oats
1 1/2 tsp baking powder
1 tsp cinnamon
4 oz dark chocolate, chopped
1/2 cup raisins
1 tbsp honey

Method
1. Heat the oven to 350 F and line two trays with baking paper.
2. Beat the butter and brown sugar until pale and fluffy, then beat in the egg and the honey.
3. Stir the flour, baking powder and cinnamon together, then fold into the butter mixture with the oats.
4. Fold in the chocolate and the raisins. The dough will be stiff.
5. Roll into 24 balls, place them well apart on the trays and flatten slightly with your palm.
6. Bake for 11 to 13 minutes until golden at the edges but still soft in the middle. Cool on the tray for five minutes before moving them.
