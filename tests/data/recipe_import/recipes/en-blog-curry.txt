Thai-ish Red Curry (Ready in 30 Minutes!)

Posted by Maria on a rainy Tuesday | 48 comments | Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 212 votes

So, a funny story. Last winter my sister visited, we had nothing in the fridge, and I threw this together from whatever was in the cupboard. She has asked for it every single visit since! I have tweaked it a bit over time and this is the version I am happiest with. Scroll down for the recipe, or keep reading for my long rambling thoughts on curry paste brands (you have been warned).

INGREDIENTS
2-3 tbsp red curry paste (depends how brave you are)
1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
1 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
1 red bell pepper, sliced
1 cup basmati rice
1 lime, cut in wedges
salt to taste

DIRECTIONS
Cook the rice. Meanwhile fry the paste in a splash of oil for one minute, add garlic and lemongrass, then the chicken. Pour in the coconut milk and simmer ten minutes. Add the pepper for the last three. Fish out the lemongrass, season with salt, serve over rice with lime wedges.
