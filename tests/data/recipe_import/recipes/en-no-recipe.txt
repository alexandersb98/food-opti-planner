Restaurant Review: Lanterns on Third Street

I wanted to love this place. The room is pretty, with paper lanterns hung over every table, and the staff were friendly in the way people are when they know the kitchen is behind. We arrived at seven on a Saturday and, after two hours of waiting, were finally given a table by the window.

The menu is short, which I usually like. I ordered the noodle soup, a 12 dollar bowl that arrived lukewarm, with a broth that tasted mostly of salt. My friend had the dumplings, six to a plate, and said they were fine. The 4 dollar side of pickles was the best thing we ate all night, crunchy and sharp.

Service was slow but kind. We left a 20 percent tip, which seemed right given the circumstances. Would I go again? Maybe on a weekday, early, with low expectations and a book.

Rating: 3 out of 5 lanterns.
